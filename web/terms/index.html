Terms of use

Updated September 20, 2026

PDSA Points is the participation and event system of the Pre-Dental Student Association at UCF. These terms describe acceptable use of the website.

Member submissions

Submit check-ins and credit amounts accurately and under your own name. Upload only relevant evidence you are entitled to share. Avoid including payment details or unrelated personal information in photos and receipts.

A submitted check-in is pending until an administrator reviews it. Points and honorary status follow the club's published requirements and approved records. Contact the club about errors or disputed records.

Leadership access

Use the Google account approved for you. Do not share access or attempt to bypass permissions. Leadership may authorize, change or revoke PDSA access as club responsibilities change. These actions do not manage or delete your Google account.

Responsible use

Do not falsify attendance, impersonate another member, disrupt the service or use member information to harass others. Public member lookup does not authorize misuse of the information displayed.

Privacy and other services

The privacy policy describes the information processed and the member information available through public lookup. Event sign-up links may lead to other services with their own terms and privacy policies.

Availability and corrections

The website may be unavailable or contain errors. Contact club leadership if you cannot check in or a record appears incorrect.

Contact and updates

Questions about this website or these terms: pdsa.ucf@gmail.com.

Updates to these terms will appear here with a revised date.
